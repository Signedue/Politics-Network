04 / Data quality and limitations

Interpretation

The dataset is bounded by observed coverage and metadata choices

The descriptive tables distinguish what was observed and included from what was unavailable or below the participation threshold.

What we can say

There are 192 recorded votes in the period, 168 with individual voting records and 24 without. Party labels use the official partyShortname value from actor biography metadata. UFG and other small-party groups have small denominators in some comparisons.

What we cannot say

Party labels are not a verified historical membership series for every vote. The 24 unavailable votes are not silently treated as absences, and small-party summaries should not be read as equally precise to larger-party summaries.

Dataset summaries are descriptive and limited to documented repository outputs.
