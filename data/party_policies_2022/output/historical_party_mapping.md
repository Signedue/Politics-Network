# Historical 2022 party-affiliation mapping

The policy corpus uses historical parties represented after the 2022 election. The politician registry retains current/observed labels unchanged; a separate person-level historical affiliation mapping is required before comparing policy texts with individual voting behaviour. UFG is a parliamentary status, not a party and is intentionally excluded from the scrape.
